Pelt Palace: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22. Applies to the Pelt Palace app for iPhone.

The short version

Pelt Palace has no accounts and no sign-in. We (SM1RK, the studio name of an individual developer) do not collect your name, email, contact details, location or anything else ourselves, and we run no servers for the game. Your progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to do that, described below.

What stays on your phone

Your league progress, unlocks, settings and purchase status are saved on your phone only. We never receive them. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows video ads between matches and offers optional bonus videos. These come from Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google's ad software on your phone may collect and use: your IP address (for a rough location), your phone's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), information about the ads you saw and how you interacted with them, and crash and performance information. Google uses this to show and measure ads. Google's own policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

  • Your choice on iPhone. The first time ads are needed, iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps. Saying no keeps the whole game working and ads still show, just less personal. You can change it any time in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
  • Where the law asks for it (for example the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent screen appears first. You can open it again from the pause menu, Shop and privacy, "Ad privacy options".
  • Age. Where a state law applies and the phone's age setting says you are under 18, or you choose not to share it, the app asks for non-personalised ads only and does not ask to track you.
  • We do not use ads aimed at children. The app is not made for children.
  • Any ad can be reported through the information button on the ad itself.

Purchases

Remove ads and the Starter pack are bought through Apple's in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment and we never see your card or Apple account details. The app only learns that a purchase exists on your Apple account so it can unlock it, and "Restore purchases" in Shop and privacy asks Apple for it again on a new phone.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data held by Google for ads is governed by Google's policy and its controls at myadcenter.google.com. To withdraw ad consent, use the iPhone Tracking setting above or the Ad privacy options button.

Children

Pelt Palace is meant for people aged 13 and over (the app's age rating is set by Apple's questionnaire). We do not knowingly collect data from children, and we collect none from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is here before an app update that needs it ships.
