Pelt Palace: Support

Contact

Email technshoes85@gmail.com. Tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We read every message.

My purchases are missing

Open the pause menu, Shop and privacy, then Restore purchases. Use the same Apple account you bought with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my progress safe?

Progress is saved on your phone after every match. Pause menu, Save code makes a code you can keep as a backup.

Ads and privacy

See the privacy policy. Change tracking in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.
